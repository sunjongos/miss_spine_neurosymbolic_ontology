# [[Endoscopic_Spine_Surgery]]
Full-endoscopic spine surgery (FESS) utilizes high-definition 4K endoscopes, continuous saline irrigation, and high-frequency radiofrequency (RF) probes to perform discectomy and decompression.

## Major Approaches
- **Transforaminal (PTED)**: Through [[Kambin_Triangle]] for lateral/foraminal disc herniation (Chapters 01, 05).
- **Interlaminar (PIED)**: Through interlaminar window for L5/S1 and central stenosis (Chapters 06, 07).
- **Unilateral Biportal Endoscopy**: Dual-portal setup. See [[Uniportal_vs_Biportal_UBE]] (Chapters 10, 13, 18).

## Safety & Complication Management
- Fluid irrigation pump pressure maintained at 30-50 mmHg to prevent intracranial pressure spikes.
- See [[Complications_and_Dural_Tears]] for dural tear protocols.
