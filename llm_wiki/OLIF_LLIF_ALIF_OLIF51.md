# [[OLIF_LLIF_ALIF_OLIF51]]
Anterior and Lateral retroperitoneal fusion strategies avoiding the spinal canal and posterior musculature.

## Modalities
- **LLIF (XLIF/DLIF)**: Trans-psoas approach (L1-L5). Requires intraoperative EMG monitoring.
- **OLIF (Oblique)**: Anterior-psoas corridor (L2-L5). Avoids psoas muscle splitting.
- **OLIF51**: Specialized oblique corridor between the iliac vessels for L5/S1 fusion.
- **ALIF**: Direct anterior retroperitoneal approach. Maximum cage footprint and lordosis restoration.

- See [[Complications_and_Dural_Tears]] for vessel isolation and left common iliac vein protection.
