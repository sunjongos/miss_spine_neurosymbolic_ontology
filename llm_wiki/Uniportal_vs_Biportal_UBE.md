# [[Uniportal_vs_Biportal_UBE]]
Detailed comparison of single-portal vs biportal endoscopic spine surgery.

| Metric | Uniportal (FESS) | Biportal (UBE) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Portals** | Single co-axial portal | Separate Viewing & Working portals |
| **Instruments** | Specialized thin endoscopic instruments | Standard open spine instruments (kerrison, burr) |
| **Decompression Scope** | Focal disc & foraminal decompression | Extensive 360° laminectomy & interbody fusion |
| **Primary Chapters** | Chapters 04, 05, 06, 14, 15 | Chapters 10, 13, 18 |
