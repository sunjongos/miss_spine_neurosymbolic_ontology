# [[Complications_and_Dural_Tears]]
Intraoperative and postoperative complication management in MISS.

## Complications & Interventions
- **Incidental Durotomy**: Endoscopic repair, fibrin glue, bed rest.
- **Vascular Injury**: Left common iliac vein protection in ALIF/OLIF51.
- **Water Intoxication**: Maintaining fluid pump pressure < 30-40 mmHg in UBE.
