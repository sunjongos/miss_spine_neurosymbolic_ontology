# [[MIS_TLIF_and_CBT]]
Minimally Invasive Transforaminal Lumbar Interbody Fusion and Cortical Bone Trajectory screwing.

## Core Concepts
- **Wiltse Corridor**: Inter-muscular plane between multifidus and longissimus muscles.
- **CBT (Cortical Bone Trajectory)**: Caudo-cephalad, medio-lateral screw trajectory targeting high-density cortical bone. Offers >30% higher pull-out strength in osteoporotic bone (Chapter 22).
- **UBE-TLIF**: Biportal endoscopic TLIF with expandable interbody cage insertion (Chapter 10).
