# [[AR_VR_MR_and_AI_in_Spine]]
Augmented Reality and Artificial Intelligence in modern spine surgery.

## Utility
- **AR / Extended Reality (HMD)**: Direct 3D overlay of patient CT/MRI onto surgeon's visual field ('X-ray vision').
- **AI Analytics**: Machine learning models for pre-operative deformity planning, automated grading, and diagnostic benchmarking against physicians.
