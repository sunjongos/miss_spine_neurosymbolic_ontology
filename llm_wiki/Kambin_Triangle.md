# [[Kambin_Triangle]]
Defined by Parviz Kambin in the 1970s-80s as the anatomical safe working zone for percutaneous transforaminal spine procedures.

## Boundaries
- **Medial**: Traversing nerve root and dural sac.
- **Lateral / Superior**: Exiting nerve root.
- **Inferior**: Superior endplate of the caudal vertebral body.

## Surgical Applications
- Utilized in transforaminal endoscopic discectomy ([[Endoscopic_Spine_Surgery]]) and percutaneous interbody fusion ([[MIS_TLIF_and_CBT]]).
- Expanded via endoscopic SAP (Superior Articular Process) resecting drills to avoid exiting root injury.
